Workspace record stores (residency policies and tenants, governor rollback packs and emergency overrides, false-positive reports, lab experiments and probe results) and the verified-artifact registry are harder to tamper with silently.

- The head anchor (`<kind>.head.json`) now carries an artifact-seal signature over its head and lost list, made by the key that signed the record. A signature that does not verify, or an unsigned anchor in a workspace with keys, is the new `HEAD_UNVERIFIED` finding, so deleting the newest record and rewriting the anchor to the older hash is caught. A workspace without keys keeps its unsigned anchors. A write over records whose anchor is gone carries `HEAD_MISSING` or `HEAD_UNANCHORED` forward instead of starting a clean anchor.
- A key set that fails its own checks (for example one flipped byte in a pinned public key) no longer reads as a keyless workspace: unsigned records stay refused and the new `KEYSET_UNVERIFIABLE` finding says why.
- A trusted read returns exactly the bytes it verified. A genuinely signed record filed under another record's id (`ID_MISMATCH`), stored twice (`DUPLICATE`) or restored from a kept revision (`SUPERSEDED`) is refused.
- Upgrade note: a store written before head anchors existed reads `HEAD_UNANCHORED` until it is anchored. Run `amc doctor` once with the vault available (`AMC_VAULT_PASSPHRASE`): its new `workspace-record-heads` check anchors each such store at its chain tip, records a `WORKSPACE_RECORD_HEAD_ANCHORED` ledger row, and backfills that row for anchors written earlier. Reads never anchor. Doctor refuses a store whose records do not verify, and fails a store whose anchor was removed after it was anchored. In residency reports `HEAD_UNANCHORED` is now a `[MEDIUM]` unanchored-history violation rather than a `[HIGH]` breach.
- The residency report reads tenants and policies only from verified records, reports a tenant with no single verified registration (or a region with more than one verified policy) as unconfigured and non-compliant instead of applying us-east-1 and the strict default, and prints a fixed sentence per integrity finding instead of the reader's error text.
- The verified-artifact registry also covers notary attestations, transform attestations, industry-pack audits, outcome contracts and experiment candidates. The transform tracker no longer marks a task ATTESTED from an unsigned or tampered attestation; the synchronous experiment run re-verifies its candidate instead of trusting the stored flag; the synchronous manual outcome attestation and the CGX context pack act only on a verified outcome contract. `amc notary verify-attest --json` also returns the verified `attestation`.
- `amc fp-cost` and `amc fp-tuning-report` are registered result commands and print a self-reported claim label, including under `AMC_CLAIM_LABELS_STRICT=1`.
