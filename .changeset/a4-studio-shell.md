Studio gains the A4 agent-project pages, behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `/console/a4` lists agent projects and offers "Start an agent project". `/console/a4Project?project=<id>` shows the stage rail, the eight steps, the conversation (Ask, Understand, Explain), an editable specification, the revision differences, the missing requirements, four lane columns (Recommendation, Implementation, Observed, Independently reviewed) with AMC's own checks in a separate Integrity panel beside them, an approvals bar (Approve, Request changes and Hold, with Deny under More), the members, a discussion thread per card and presence. The page refreshes by polling every 4 seconds, or every second while a build runs, and pauses while the tab is hidden. Buttons follow the server's `readiness.allowed` and show its reason codes when disabled; the server re-checks every action. Every write carries a request id, so a retry after a lost response returns the recorded result. Decisions carry the gate's open sequence and the two digests the page showed. Editing a specification on a stale head shows the head's changes and offers "Apply my changes on top" or "Reload". Claim labels are the server's, printed as received. Without the preview, the home card and the A4 nav link are absent and both pages show "A4 preview is not enabled in this workspace". The `/api/v1/a4` routes land separately (P1-57); until they do, the pages show that notice.
