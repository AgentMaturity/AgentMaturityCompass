Studio gains the A4 agent-project pages, behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `/console/a4` lists agent projects and offers "Start an agent project". `/console/a4Project?project=<id>` shows the stage rail, the eight steps, the conversation (Ask, Understand, Explain), an editable specification, the revision differences, the missing requirements, four lane columns (Recommendation, Implementation, Observed, Independently reviewed) with AMC's own checks in a separate Integrity panel beside them, an approvals bar (Approve, Request changes and Hold, with Deny under More), the members, a discussion thread per card and presence. The page refreshes by polling every 4 seconds, or every second while a build runs, and pauses while the tab is hidden; a poll whose head names another project, or that moves backwards, is refused. Presence is also taken from each heartbeat's answer. Buttons follow the server's `readiness.allowed` and show its reason codes when disabled; the server re-checks every action. Because `readiness.allowed` does not encode the step order, Save answers, Confirm, Run Understand, "Yes, that's it", "Correct this", Explain, Propose, Build, Review and the two gate requests are also disabled with `A4_STEP_ORDER` on another stage's view and whenever the project is not at the step Studio requires (Understand and its confirmation at `asked`, Explain at `understood`, Propose at `explained`, Build at `direction_approved`, Review at `built`, the direction request at `proposed`, the completion request at `reviewed`). The two gate requests are also disabled with `A4_GATE_OPEN` while that kind's gate reads `PENDING` or `QUORUM_MET`, and with `A4_GATE_DENIED` after a Deny of the current revision. One write runs at a time, and every write carries a request id, so a retry after a lost response returns the recorded result; while a write is unconfirmed no other write is sent, and a refused retry leaves it marked unconfirmed and tells the user to reload and check before repeating it.

A page load re-reads, three times at most, until the specification it shows and every live gate belong to the same revision. Approve and Deny carry the gate's open sequence (`requestedSeq`, accepted only when the head has reached it) and the two digests of the gate the approver reviewed; Request changes carries the gate's open sequence, and the page checks the same pinned review before sending it. Deciding is disabled and refused unless the specification shown is that gate's revision, and while the specification card shows the member's own unsaved draft (from the first keystroke until the draft is proposed or discarded with "Discard my edits"); a gate or readiness without a published digest cannot be decided. Approve is disabled with `SOD_VIOLATION` for a member the gate excludes (its requester, the revision's author, or for a completion gate a builder), unless Studio reports that self-approval is open; Deny stays available to them, as Studio allows, and the bar lists them as "Cannot approve (separation of duties); may still deny". Approve, Deny, Request changes, Hold, Resume and Acknowledge need a reason, and an empty one is refused on the page. If a refresh shows a different gate or readiness, Approve, Deny, Request changes and Complete are disabled until the user presses "Show r<N>". Only direction and completion gates are decided from the approvals bar; an open gate-policy change is listed as decided elsewhere. The bar shows the viewed stage's direction and completion gates as readiness reports them, with readiness's status printed as received (`PENDING`, `QUORUM_MET`, `STALE`, `DENIED`, `EXPIRED`, `CONSUMED` or `CHANGES_REQUESTED`), its approvals of the required count and the gate's decisions; only a gate readiness reads `PENDING` can be approved, denied or sent back, and only one it reads `QUORUM_MET` can be completed. The stage's earlier gates are listed under "Earlier gates", each bound to its revision with the status Studio gives it. Decisions readiness lists as stale show its reason codes as received, and only `GATE_STALE` ones, which its quorum excludes, are struck through. Complete is not offered for Activate's completion gate, because its response carries a one-time token that only the Activate card may show; Activate's direction gate is completed like any other.

Until a stage producer is registered, Understand and Explain record what the member writes as their own self-reported statement, and the page shows what was sent beside Studio's receipt, never as AMC's understanding. "Yes, that's it" is disabled until a statement is shown and binds the head it was recorded at. "Correct this" sends nothing: it puts the statement back in the Understand field and opens "Change my answers", and the member runs Understand again. Unanswered questions are the open fields of the Ask form; the answers of the newest revision recorded at the viewed stage (the revision Studio's Ask merges into, also after a reopen) are listed read-only with their source, carried and inferred ones with Confirm, and "Change my answers" holds their fields. Save answers sends only the answers the user changed, edits a non-text answer as JSON, and binds the head the first edit began on: if another member changes an answer being edited, Save is held until the user discards their edits, and when any other write moved the head nothing is re-sent: the page names the project's new revision and step, and the next press of Save binds the head it now shows. While a stage has no registered questions, Ask offers one free-text `notes` answer. Ask says that saving answers records a new revision, which supersedes the current revision's gates, and returns the stage to Ask. The specification editor never shows or sends the answers Studio carries, refuses an `answers` key, sends no parent for a first revision, and says when it starts from another stage's revision, naming the stage's own newest revision when one exists. Another stage's view shows the current specification read-only. Studio takes a proposal only at step `explained`, so the editor says "Editing after Propose creates a new revision" only at that step and otherwise names the step the project is at; until Studio accepts a re-proposal from `proposed`, a member asked for changes, or whose gate was denied, reaches a new revision by saving an answer under Ask, and the editor says so. A proposal Studio refuses with `A4_STALE_HEAD` or `A4_STEP_ORDER` shows the head's changes and the member's own. "Apply my changes on top" is offered only while the head is at step `explained` of the viewed stage; otherwise the card says why and offers only "Reload". A proposal is never re-sent unseen: when the head moved without a new revision, the card says so and the member applies their changes on top. "Apply my changes on top" merges the editor's current text, compared with the revision that text was edited from, so a second attempt never reverts another member's change. Paths that both the editor and the head changed (the same path, or one inside the other, unless both wrote the same value) are listed and nothing is sent until the member presses "Apply mine over theirs" for that list, which writes the editor's value there. Pasted text in the Understand, Explain, Build, Review and comment fields is retained until the project's blob key is destroyed, and each says so. The specification and the answers are stored as written in the project's record, not in its encrypted blob store, so destroying the blob key does not erase them; the editor and the Ask form say so and ask the member not to paste personal data or secrets there. Acknowledge is offered only on the waiting items Studio accepts an acknowledgement for (`lineage.independent_approvals`, `sod.self_provisioned`, `regulatory_sources_status`, `deployment.amc_check`, `plan_unresolved`). The create form takes an agent id only in Studio's form (lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`, at most 128 characters) and refuses a blank project name before sending; the Understand, Explain, Build and Review fields hold at most 65,536 characters. A `card` URL parameter that names no card of the viewed stage is ignored for presence and polling. Comments are threaded per stage and card, and a thread opens when its first comment arrives; a comment's author is shown by username while they are a member, otherwise by the principal key Studio returns. Actions clear only the drafts they used, and a refresh keeps the sections the user opened or closed. The claim strip shows only the server's labels, from the latest completed load. Without the preview, the home card and the A4 nav link are absent and both pages show "A4 preview is not enabled in this workspace"; any other refusal is shown as an error.
