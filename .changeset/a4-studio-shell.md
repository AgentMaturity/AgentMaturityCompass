Studio gains the A4 agent-project pages, behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `/console/a4` lists agent projects and offers "Start an agent project". `/console/a4Project?project=<id>` shows the stage rail, the eight steps, the conversation (Ask, Understand, Explain), an editable specification, the revision differences, the missing requirements, four lane columns (Recommendation, Implementation, Observed, Independently reviewed) with AMC's own checks in a separate Integrity panel beside them, an approvals bar (Approve, Request changes and Hold, with Deny under More), the members, a discussion thread per card and presence. The page refreshes by polling every 4 seconds, or every second while a build runs, and pauses while the tab is hidden; a poll whose head names another project, or that moves backwards, is refused. Presence is also taken from each heartbeat's answer. Buttons follow the server's `readiness.allowed` and show its reason codes when disabled; the server re-checks every action. One write runs at a time, and every write carries a request id, so a retry after a lost response returns the recorded result; while a write is unconfirmed no other write is sent, and a refused retry leaves it marked unconfirmed and tells the user to reload and check before repeating it.

A page load re-reads, three times at most, until the specification it shows and every live gate belong to the same revision. Approve and Deny carry the gate's open sequence (`requestedSeq`, accepted only when the head has reached it) and the two digests of the gate the approver reviewed; Request changes carries the gate's open sequence, and the page checks the same pinned review before sending it. Deciding is disabled and refused unless the specification shown is that gate's revision, and while the specification card shows the member's own unsaved draft (from the first keystroke until the draft is proposed or discarded with "Discard my edits"); a gate or readiness without a published digest cannot be decided. Approve, Deny, Request changes, Hold, Resume and Acknowledge need a reason, and an empty one is refused on the page. If a refresh shows a different gate or readiness, Approve, Deny, Request changes and Complete are disabled until the user presses "Show r<N>". Only direction and completion gates are decided from the approvals bar; an open gate-policy change is listed as decided elsewhere. Decisions Studio lists as stale show its reason codes as received, and only `GATE_STALE` ones, which its quorum excludes, are struck through. Complete is not offered for Activate's completion gate, because its response carries a one-time token that only the Activate card may show; Activate's direction gate is completed like any other.

Until a stage producer is registered, Understand and Explain record what the member writes as their own self-reported statement, and the page shows what was sent beside Studio's receipt, never as AMC's understanding. "Yes, that's it" is disabled until a statement is shown and binds the head it was recorded at. "Correct this" sends nothing: it puts the statement back in the Understand field and opens "Change my answers", and the member runs Understand again. Unanswered questions are the open fields of the Ask form; answers recorded at the viewed stage are listed read-only with their source, carried and inferred ones with Confirm, and "Change my answers" holds their fields. Save answers sends only the answers the user changed, edits a non-text answer as JSON, and binds the head the first edit began on: if another member changes an answer being edited, Save is held until the user discards their edits, and a write that changed none of those answers is re-sent once on the new head. The specification editor never shows or sends the answers Studio carries, refuses an `answers` key, sends no parent for a first revision, and says when it starts from another stage's revision. Another stage's view shows the current specification read-only. Editing a specification on a stale head shows the head's changes and offers "Apply my changes on top" or "Reload"; when only a comment or other non-revision change moved the head, the proposal is re-sent once on the new head. "Apply my changes on top" merges the editor's current text. Pasted text in the specification, Understand, Explain, Build, Review and comment fields is retained until the project's blob key is destroyed, and each says so. Comments are threaded per stage and card, and a thread opens when its first comment arrives. Actions clear only the drafts they used, and a refresh keeps the sections the user opened or closed. The claim strip shows only the server's labels, from the latest completed load. Without the preview, the home card and the A4 nav link are absent and both pages show "A4 preview is not enabled in this workspace"; any other refusal is shown as an error. The `/api/v1/a4` routes land separately (P1-57); until they do, the pages show that notice.
