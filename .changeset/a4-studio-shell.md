Studio gains the A4 agent-project pages, behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `/console/a4` lists agent projects and offers "Start an agent project". `/console/a4Project?project=<id>` shows the stage rail, the eight steps, the conversation (Ask, Understand, Explain), an editable specification, the revision differences, the missing requirements, four lane columns (Recommendation, Implementation, Observed, Independently reviewed) with AMC's own checks in a separate Integrity panel beside them, an approvals bar (Approve, Request changes and Hold, with Deny under More), the members, a discussion thread per card and presence. The page refreshes by polling every 4 seconds, or every second while a build runs, and pauses while the tab is hidden; a poll whose head does not name the project, or that moves backwards, is refused. Buttons follow the server's `readiness.allowed` and show its reason codes when disabled; the server re-checks every action. One write runs at a time, and every write carries a request id, so a retry after a lost response returns the recorded result; a refused retry leaves the write marked unconfirmed and tells the user to reload and check before repeating it.

Decisions carry the gate's open sequence (`requestedSeq`) and the two digests of the gate the approver reviewed. If a refresh shows a different gate or readiness, Approve, Deny, Request changes and Complete are disabled until the user presses "Show r<N>". Decisions Studio does not count are struck through with their reason codes. Complete is not offered on Activate, because its response carries a one-time token that only the Activate card may show. Understand's and Explain's responses are shown in the conversation card, and "Yes, that's it" and "Correct this" are disabled until a reflection is shown; they bind the head that reflection belongs to. Comments are threaded per stage and card. Another stage's view shows the current specification read-only. Editing a specification on a stale head shows the head's changes and offers "Apply my changes on top" or "Reload"; when only a comment or other non-revision change moved the head, the proposal is re-sent once on the new head. Actions clear only the drafts they used. The claim strip shows only the server's labels, from the latest load. Without the preview, the home card and the A4 nav link are absent and both pages show "A4 preview is not enabled in this workspace". The `/api/v1/a4` routes land separately (P1-57); until they do, the pages show that notice.
