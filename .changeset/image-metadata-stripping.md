Input images no longer carry their metadata to the model provider (PXB-42). When an image becomes the attachment row that a request reads, AMC removes EXIF (GPS included), IPTC, XMP and comments by keeping only what renders the image: JPEG segments other than APPn and COM (JFIF APP0 and Adobe APP14 are kept), walked through every scan to the first EOI, with anything after EOI (such as an appended MPF image) dropped; PNG `IHDR`, `PLTE`, `IDAT`, `IEND` and the colour, transparency, physical-size and APNG chunks, with any other ancillary chunk dropped and an unknown critical chunk refused; WebP `VP8 `, `VP8L`, `VP8X`, `ALPH`, `ANIM`, `ANMF` and `ICCP` chunks, with the VP8X metadata flags cleared and the RIFF size rewritten; GIF image descriptors, graphic-control extensions and animation-looping application extensions. Kept segments and chunks are copied byte for byte. An image in an unsupported format, a truncated or malformed container, or a PNG chunk outside the image data whose CRC does not match is refused with a typed reason (`ImageMetadataStripError`: `unsupported-format` or `malformed-container`) and nothing is recorded; the original is never sent instead. The signed inbox keeps the original bytes, and the attachment row records `sourceSha256` (the original) and `sentSha256` (what was sent), so the receipt shows the change. The per-image size cap at that row applies to the stripped bytes. History recorded before this change still verifies against its unstripped originals.
