Input images no longer carry their metadata to the model provider (PXB-42). When an image becomes the attachment row that a request reads, AMC removes EXIF (GPS included), IPTC, XMP and comments: JPEG APPn segments other than JFIF (APP0) and Adobe (APP14), and COM segments; PNG `tEXt`, `zTXt`, `iTXt`, `eXIf` and `tIME` chunks; WebP `EXIF` and `XMP ` chunks, with their VP8X flags cleared and the RIFF size rewritten; GIF comment extensions and every application extension except animation looping. Pixel data and every other segment or chunk are kept byte for byte. An image in an unsupported format, a truncated or malformed container, or a PNG chunk outside the image data whose CRC does not match is refused with a typed reason (`ImageMetadataStripError`: `unsupported-format` or `malformed-container`) and nothing is recorded; the original is never sent instead. The signed inbox keeps the original bytes, and the attachment row records `sourceSha256` (the original) and `sentSha256` (what was sent), so the receipt shows the change. The per-image size cap at that row applies to the stripped bytes. History recorded before this change still verifies against its unstripped originals.
