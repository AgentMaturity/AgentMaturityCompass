Judge results now say what actually happened (GAP-WP11). `LLMJudgeEngine` forwards its configured model, temperature and token cap to the production judge transport and records them on every result as `judge`. Only settings a caller omits take the transport defaults. A malformed, non-object or out-of-range judge reply is now a result with `status: "invalid"` and `score: null` instead of a numeric 0. Invalid results are never cached, and the cache key includes the judge settings. A missing `confidence` stays `null`, and an explicit `confidence: 0` stays 0. Category summaries, overall scores and `AMCJudgeTestRunner` average scored metrics only, and an invalid metric fails its test. The judge client counts transport attempts, including failed ones, and charges no cost for them. `LLMApiRequestError` carries the attempt count.

`agents/LLMJudge.comparePairwise` calls the configured judge adapter in `llm` mode and rejects when it is missing or returns no valid verdict. Only `simulated` mode uses the length heuristic. An `llm` evaluation whose score is off the rubric scale now rejects instead of being normalized. `amc agent harness` prints the measured probe score separately and labels its projected score and improvement "(simulated, not executed)". Its result carries `measuredScore`, `scoreKind`, and `simulated: true` on simulated capability probes. When a runtime's claimed levels fail, the harness no longer waits for pasted JSON on a non-terminal stdin. Claims that a person pasted at the prompt add `FLAG_CLAIM_HUMAN_PASTED` to their questions. The hallucination judge rejects an unparseable reply with `LlmJudgeInvalidResponseError` instead of reporting no findings. `detectHallucinations` returns `evaluated: false, passed: null` when it had neither grounding context nor a judge. `createProductionJudgeFn` accepts and forwards judge settings.
