Adds catalog governance checks; no content is promoted by this change (P1-34). Every catalog record is still `experimental`, and the rules below are a draft awaiting Sid's approval (docs/catalog/GOVERNANCE.md).

- **Review records and support.** `catalog/reviews/<subject>/<version>/<record>.json` holds human review records, each signed by its reviewer's own Ed25519 key under the new `catalog-review` key purpose (AMC never holds one; a trust-list entry that pins a `catalog-review` key for any other purpose too is refused for every purpose, in AMC and the standalone verifier). `effectiveSupport` verifies records at the server's clock against trust it loads only from the operator's AMC home (`AMC_HOME`, else `~/.config/amc`; without `trust/trust-roots.json` nothing is promoted: "trust roots not configured"): the signed trust list, pinned roots, m-of-n, anti-rollback and distrust. It is the only source of promotions: a record counts only when it covers the control's current content digest, is not dated after the clock or before its key's validity, verifies under a key a list entry pins for `catalog-review` (never a `--pubkey` pin, workspace key or `--allow-unpinned`; revoked, distrusted, expired and wrong-purpose keys refused, and so is a key any loaded list pins for another purpose), names that entry's subject, that subject is on the D-08 roster (keyed by subject, listing domains and at least one git identity), none of the reviewer's identities (subject, roster git identities, declared name; compared after NFKC, removing default-ignorable code points, whitespace collapsing, case folding and dropping an email's `+tag` and trailing domain dot) authored the control (`git log --follow`), any confirmed F5 verdict has a confirmation row, and the review is current. The quorum needs two distinct people (reviewers sharing any identifier are one), one credentialed for the control's domain, a credentialed legal reviewer when the control cites law and an AI-draft confirmation naming the control (`<id>@<version>` or its digest), with no objection and no open re-verify item. A verified record shows who approved what and when, not that the content is right.
- **Every view reads the result.** `supportStanding` is the one pure function that lets declared reviewed or qualified support stand: only a frozen promotion `effectiveSupport` minted, naming reviewers and the digest of every counted record, for the control's current digest, reviewed on or before the plan's `asOf` and not yet due. Hand-built or empty promotions are refused. `compilePlan` takes `promotions` as an input, judges them at the plan's `asOf` and records only the ones it accepted in the plan (so in its digest); it stays pure, and with no promotions every row is experimental and the plan digest is unchanged. Activation's policy receipts, the A4 conformance and why-view rows and the OSCAL catalog (support, the review block, the approver and each citation's legal review) show an approved review as `pending` with no expert, credential or dates, withhold the approver and show a citation's legal review as `unverified` unless support stood. A citation's legal review in the A4 why-view and the OSCAL citation is exactly one of `none`, `recorded` or `unverified`, passed through raw to the A4 API and to any view that renders it. For the shipped catalog nothing changes: all 62 controls are experimental with pending reviews, so sealed A4 conformance statements recompute identically. A review falls due 30 days after its signed date when the control cites a US state, EU guidance (or an unregistered EU instrument) or an Indian draft, otherwise after 90 days, never later than a cited S5 register entry's own next review. `npm run check:catalog-currency` (CI, `--json`, `--as-of` only moves due dates ahead) fails on reviewed or qualified content whose support does not stand and on `GOV_REVERIFY_OPEN`, and warns on reviews falling due, unverified approved declarations and register entries due a status review. The YAML, the control digest, the lock and stored results never change.
- **Changelog.** `catalog/CHANGELOG.md` is append-only. `npm run check:catalog-changelog` (CI) fails a change to the catalog digest unless it appends exactly one entry naming the new catalog version and digest and every added, changed or retired control, with review record ids for reviewed or qualified content, and prints the entry it expects. A content change therefore also bumps `catalog/catalog.yaml`'s version. Removing a control outright is refused; retire it.
- **Tracked lists.** `catalog/governance/reverify-before-publishing.yaml` (15 facts, all open), `f5-confirmations.yaml` (none confirmed) and `standards-licences.md` (costs for Sid to fill). The catalog loader accepts these files and `CHANGELOG.md`; they change no digest.
