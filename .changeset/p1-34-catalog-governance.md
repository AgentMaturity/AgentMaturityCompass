Adds catalog governance checks; no content is promoted by this change (P1-34). Every catalog record is still `experimental`, and the rules below are a draft awaiting Sid's approval (docs/catalog/GOVERNANCE.md).

- **Review records.** `catalog/reviews/<subject>/<version>/<record>.json` holds human review records, each signed by its reviewer's own Ed25519 key. `verifyReviewRecord` accepts one only when a signed trust list pins that key for the new `catalog-review` key purpose (the operator's m-of-n root policy applies; a `--pubkey` pin, workspace keys or `--allow-unpinned` never do), the record names that trust-list entry's subject as its reviewer, the reviewer authored neither the subject nor the change, credential and conflict declarations are non-blank and any confirmed F5 verdict has a confirmation row. Forged, unpinned, self-signed and self-reviewed records are unverified and move nothing; a record whose subject has changed since is expired. `meetsReviewQuorum` needs two distinct pinned reviewers, one credentialed for the subject's domain in the D-08 expert roster (none is named yet), a credentialed legal reviewer for citations and a confirming approval for AI-drafted content; any valid objection blocks it. AMC never holds a `catalog-review` private key. A verified record shows who approved what and when, not that the content is right.
- **Support and currency.** `compilePlan` compiles reviewed or qualified content as `experimental` in that plan's requirement row, with the reason, unless its review is current and a verified quorum supports it (`effectiveSupport`); no caller passes review authority yet, so every row stays experimental. A reviewed control falls due 30 days after its review when it cites a US state, EU guidance (or an unregistered EU instrument) or an Indian draft, otherwise after 90 days, and never later than a cited S5 register entry's own next review. Currency only makes support stricter; a missing, unparsable or future review date is stale, never current. `npm run check:catalog-currency` (CI, `--as-of`, `--json`) warns 14 days ahead and fails on overdue, stale or unverified reviewed or qualified content and on `GOV_REVERIFY_OPEN`; a stale review on other content is a warning; it lists register entries due a status review. The YAML, the control digest, the lock and stored results never change.
- **Changelog.** `catalog/CHANGELOG.md` is append-only. `npm run check:catalog-changelog` (CI) fails a change to the catalog digest unless it appends exactly one entry naming the new catalog version and digest and every added, changed or retired control, with review record ids for reviewed or qualified content, and prints the entry it expects. A content change therefore also bumps `catalog/catalog.yaml`'s version. Removing a control outright is refused; retire it.
- **Tracked lists.** `catalog/governance/reverify-before-publishing.yaml` (15 facts, all open), `f5-confirmations.yaml` (none confirmed) and `standards-licences.md` (costs for Sid to fill). The catalog loader accepts these files and `CHANGELOG.md`; they change no digest.
