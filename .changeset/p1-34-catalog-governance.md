Adds catalog governance checks; no content is promoted by this change (P1-34). Every catalog record is still `experimental`, and the rules below are a draft awaiting Sid's approval (docs/catalog/GOVERNANCE.md).

- **Review records.** `catalog/reviews/<subject>/<version>/<record>.json` holds human review records. `verifyReviewRecord` accepts one only when its reviewer's own signed decision, bound to the request digest, sits on an approval request whose intent covers the record's subject digest; it refuses records declaring an agent identity, decisions by a user with the `AGENT` role, self-review, blank credentials or conflict declarations, and confirmed F5 verdicts with no confirmation row. A record whose subject has changed since is expired. `meetsReviewQuorum` needs two distinct humans, one credentialed for the subject's domain in the D-08 expert roster (none is named yet), a credentialed legal reviewer for citations and a confirming approval for AI-drafted content; any verified objection blocks it. A verified record shows who approved what and when, not that the content is right; workspace-key signatures are a local audit trail.
- **Currency.** A reviewed control falls due 30 days after its review when it cites a US state, EU guidance (or an unregistered EU instrument) or an Indian draft, otherwise after 90 days, and never later than a cited S5 register entry's own next review. `npm run check:catalog-currency` (CI, `--as-of`, `--json`) warns 14 days ahead, fails on overdue or unschedulable reviewed or qualified content and on `GOV_REVERIFY_OPEN`, and lists register entries due a status review. `compilePlan` now compiles overdue or unschedulable reviewed content as `experimental` in that plan's requirement row, with the reason; the YAML, the control digest, the lock and stored results are unchanged.
- **Changelog.** `catalog/CHANGELOG.md` is append-only. `npm run check:catalog-changelog` (CI) fails a change to the catalog digest unless it appends exactly one entry naming the new catalog version and digest and every added, changed or retired control, with review record ids for reviewed or qualified content, and prints the entry it expects. A content change therefore also bumps `catalog/catalog.yaml`'s version. Removing a control outright is refused; retire it.
- **Tracked lists.** `catalog/governance/reverify-before-publishing.yaml` (15 facts, all open), `f5-confirmations.yaml` (none confirmed) and `standards-licences.md` (costs for Sid to fill). The catalog loader accepts these files and `CHANGELOG.md`; they change no digest.
