Adds catalog governance checks; no content is promoted by this change (P1-34). Every catalog record is still `experimental`, and the rules below are a draft awaiting Sid's approval (docs/catalog/GOVERNANCE.md).

- **Review records and support.** `catalog/reviews/<subject>/<version>/<record>.json` holds human review records, each signed by its reviewer's own Ed25519 key under the new `catalog-review` key purpose (AMC never holds one). `effectiveSupport` is the only function that lets a control's declared reviewed or qualified support stand: it loads the operator's signed trust lists (pinned roots, m-of-n, anti-rollback, distrust) at the server's clock and counts a record only when it covers the control's current content digest, verifies under a key a list entry pins for `catalog-review` (never a `--pubkey` pin, workspace key or `--allow-unpinned`; revoked, distrusted, expired and wrong-purpose keys refused), names that entry's subject as its reviewer, the reviewer is not an author, it is not dated after the clock or before the key's validity, any confirmed F5 verdict has a confirmation row, and the review is current. The quorum then needs two distinct reviewers, one credentialed for the control's domain in the D-08 roster (none is named yet), a credentialed legal reviewer when the control cites law and a confirmed draft for AI-drafted content, with no objection and no open re-verify item. Forged, unpinned, self-signed, self-reviewed and stale records move nothing. A verified record shows who approved what and when, not that the content is right.
- **Every view reads that result.** `compilePlan` rows, activation's policy receipts, the A4 conformance and why-view rows and the OSCAL catalog's `support` prop take support and the shown review status from `effectiveSupport`: an approved review the YAML declares shows as `pending` (and a citation's legal review as `unverified`) unless support stood. No caller passes review authority yet, so every row stays experimental. A review falls due 30 days after its signed date when the control cites a US state, EU guidance (or an unregistered EU instrument) or an Indian draft, otherwise after 90 days, never later than a cited S5 register entry's own next review. `npm run check:catalog-currency` (CI, `--json`, `--as-of` only ahead of today) fails on reviewed or qualified content whose support does not stand and on `GOV_REVERIFY_OPEN`, and warns on reviews falling due, unverified approved declarations and register entries due a status review. The YAML, the control digest, the lock and stored results never change.
- **Changelog.** `catalog/CHANGELOG.md` is append-only. `npm run check:catalog-changelog` (CI) fails a change to the catalog digest unless it appends exactly one entry naming the new catalog version and digest and every added, changed or retired control, with review record ids for reviewed or qualified content, and prints the entry it expects. A content change therefore also bumps `catalog/catalog.yaml`'s version. Removing a control outright is refused; retire it.
- **Tracked lists.** `catalog/governance/reverify-before-publishing.yaml` (15 facts, all open), `f5-confirmations.yaml` (none confirmed) and `standards-licences.md` (costs for Sid to fill). The catalog loader accepts these files and `CHANGELOG.md`; they change no digest.
