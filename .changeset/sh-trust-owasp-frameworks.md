OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 (`OWASP_LLM`, alias `owasp-llm`) and OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026 (`OWASP_AGENTIC`, ASI01–ASI10, aliases `owasp-agentic`, `asi`) are evidence-bound compliance frameworks (SH-TRUST-04), each entry bound to whole assurance packs and native audit rows (`AUTHORIZATION_RECORD`, `NATIVE_SHELL_CONFINEMENT`, `DELEGATION_SETTLED`) with its rationale in the description; every entry is NOT_EVALUATED until bound evidence exists. Both are community guidance and carry no verified source (genai.owasp.org is not on the official host list); the family review says so, and the agentic ids must be re-verified against the OWASP release document. `owasp-llm` used to alias the OWASP API Top 10. The 2023-numbered surfaces (`amc score owasp-llm`, the `owaspGenAI` pack, the `excessive-agency` and `overreliance` pack titles) now name their edition. `amc compliance init --merge` adds built-in frameworks a workspace's signed maps lack without overwriting operator mappings, then re-signs.
