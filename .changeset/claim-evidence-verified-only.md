A claim line says `Evidence: sufficient` only when the evidence is verified: the claim kind is observed (or independently reviewed). Before, any result with at least one record printed `Evidence: sufficient`, even when nothing behind it was verified: `amc audit binder create` on an empty workspace, `amc badge`, `amc passport` results, attestations, imports, the control-surface scorers and results stored by AMC 1.x. These now print `Claim: Self-reported · … · Evidence: incomplete`, and `--json`, MCP, the API and Studio carry `statusDimensions.evidence: "incomplete"`. Compliance categories and control results whose admitted records are not all observed also read `incomplete`. A regulated result whose evidence is not verified no longer shows `pass`: it reads `Result: not evaluated (self-reported answers or records cannot pass a regulated control)`, the reason text that self-answers already printed, now worded to cover records. D-03 allows a self-reported regulated pass when evidence is provided, but the v1 contracts cannot express it (a control-result `pass` requires `sufficient` evidence, and no evidence state means "self-reported, attached, not independently verified"), so AMC emits no such pass until a v2 contract adds that state. A failed or unpinned verification still reads `untrusted`.
