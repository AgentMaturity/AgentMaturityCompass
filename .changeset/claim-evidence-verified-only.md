A claim line says `Evidence: sufficient` only when the evidence is verified (the claim kind is observed or independently reviewed) or under the D-03 rule below. Before, any result with at least one record printed `Evidence: sufficient`, even when nothing behind it was verified: `amc audit binder create` on an empty workspace, `amc badge`, `amc passport` results, attestations, imports, the control-surface scorers and results stored by AMC 1.x. These now print `Claim: Self-reported · … · Evidence: incomplete`, and `--json`, MCP, the API and Studio carry `statusDimensions.evidence: "incomplete"`. Compliance categories and control results whose admitted records are not all observed also read `incomplete` unless they pass under D-03. D-03 is kept, for AMC's own signed records only: a regulated self-reported result whose records the workspace flow captured and verified (at least one record, and no other rule withholding the pass) still shows `pass`, with `statusDimensions.evidence: "sufficient"` because control-result v1 requires it for a pass. It stays unmistakably self-reported: `claimKind` is `self_reported` in the result, inside the signed CONTROL_RESULT bytes (relabelling it breaks the signature) and in every claim line, and the label reads `Result: pass (self-reported, evidence attached, not independently verified)`. A legacy 1.x, imported or unsigned self-reported result, or one without records, shows no pass: `Result: not evaluated (self-reported answers or records cannot satisfy a regulated control)`; that reason text, which numeric self-answers also print, was "self-reported answers cannot pass a regulated control". A D-03 pass satisfies no total or score: a fleet, report or compliance-category total that contains one is not evaluated with evidence `incomplete`, and the compliance coverage score (and Comply's surface score) credits only observed or independently reviewed passes. Observed results are unchanged. A failed or unpinned verification still reads `untrusted`.
