Docs: a draft procurement clause library (`docs/procurement-clauses.json`, rendered to `docs/PROCUREMENT_CLAUSES.md` by `scripts/gen-procurement-clauses.mjs --write`; `--check` fails on drift). Twelve clauses with stable ids cover evidence delivery, independent verification, claim vocabulary, attestation scope, testing and monitoring, change notice, incident notice, data use and exit, audit access, sub-processors, RBI retained decisions and the DORA register of information. Each clause names the AMC artifact that supports it, or says it is not provided by AMC, and cites its sources with the date they were checked (OMB M-25-22, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 and RBI's 2025 outsourcing directions). Every clause is experimental: agent-drafted, not reviewed by counsel and not legal advice. The page is listed on the docs site.
