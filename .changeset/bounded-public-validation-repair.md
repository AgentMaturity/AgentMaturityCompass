Add optional bounded repair after failed operator-selected public native validation. Repairs reuse the frozen check plan and existing governed filesystem tools, restrict access to explicitly selected files, and preserve signed checks, attempts, usage, and stop reasons. Existing run, chat, ACP, and TypeScript SDK options expose the policy. Repair is off by default; exhaustion never becomes a validation pass, and public validation does not establish independent correctness.
