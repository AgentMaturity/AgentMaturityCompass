Adds `amc export oscal` (catalog, profile, assessment results) with a loss report; results keep their claim kinds and never turn not-evaluated into satisfied. Experimental. `amc export oscal --out <dir> [--plan <plan.json>] [--results <file>]` writes OSCAL 1.2.3 JSON: `catalog.json` from the shipped Regulated Control Catalog, `profile.json` from a compiled plan (`amc catalog compile`), `assessment-results.json` from P1-11 control results, and `oscal-loss-report.json` (`amc.oscal-loss/1`) listing every AMC field OSCAL could not carry natively, with counts. The output is evidence of conformity, never a compliance statement. `docs/exports/OSCAL.md` lists every OSCAL field used and every AMC prop.

- The profile includes exactly the plan's applicable controls (`include-controls` by id, no modifications). Excluded controls with their reasons, unresolved controls, conflicts and unsupported controls are AMC props and loss-report entries; the plan and lockfile digests are metadata props and back-matter resources. A plan with no applicable control writes no profile.
- Each control result becomes an observation with its claim kind, five status dimensions and evidence references by digest as AMC props. Only an AMC pass with claim kind `observed` becomes a `satisfied` finding, and a fail a `not-satisfied` one. A not-evaluated result, or a pass that is only self-reported, gets an observation and no finding, and a loss-report entry. A crosswalk never satisfies its target.
- `--plan` must hash to its digest and its `CONTROL_PLAN` signature must verify against this workspace's auditor keys (a local audit trail, not portable trust), and the shipped catalog must match the plan's lockfile. `--results` needs `--plan`; the whole file is refused unless every result has the P1-11 shape and its own digest, names the shipped catalog's control version and digest, was evaluated under that plan with the plan's applicability, and is coherent (synthetic results are never a pass or fail; a pass needs applicable and sufficient). Control results are not signed yet: their digest is unkeyed, and the assessment results say so.
- Without results, no assessment-results file is written and the command prints `not evaluated: no control results` (exit 0). `--out` under `.amc/` is refused, and so is an `--out` holding a profile or assessment results from an earlier export that this export would not replace.
- Ids are version 5 UUIDs under one fixed namespace, props use the namespace `https://agentmaturity.co/spec/oscal/v0`, files are canonical JSON, and `last-modified` comes from the inputs, so re-exporting the same inputs gives byte-identical files. `amc export grc` and its SARIF output are unchanged.
