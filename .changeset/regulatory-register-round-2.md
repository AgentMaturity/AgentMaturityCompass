The regulatory register (`src/compliance/regulatory/register.json`) grows from 17 to 176 instruments across 20 jurisdictions: 108 verified and 68 unverified, all last reviewed on 2026-10-03, so `npm run check:regulatory-currency` fails from 2 January 2027 until they are re-reviewed. Every key date now carries the official `url` it was read from (or sought on, when it is unverified) and its `retrievedAt` date, and register entries may name the industry packs they affect and the instruments they supersede (`supersedes`, `supersedesInstruments`), mark withdrawn or revoked instruments (`taskStatus` under status `superseded`) and flag retrieval observations that are not legal dates. China, Brazil, India and Japan entries are unverified and assert no verified obligation; some of their key dates are marked as read from an official source. The register's official-source host list (`policy.officialHosts`, read by `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_HOSTS` in `src/compliance/regulatory/index.ts` and by the currency check) grows from 27 to 57 hosts, including a self-regulatory organisation (finra.org, cited only for its own rules) and intergovernmental publishers (oecd.org, oecd.ai, coe.int); the additions await expert ratification. `EU_AI_ACT_TIMELINE` adds `publicAuthorityHighRiskDeadline` (2 August 2030, Art. 111(2) as amended) and `annexXLargeScaleIt` (31 December 2030, Art. 111(1)). Register bases cite program receipts as `program-records/2026-10-03/<path>`, a name for records held outside the repository. The register is agent-drafted and stays experimental until a named expert reviews it: `verified` means the fact was read from an official source, not that an expert approved it, and it is not legal advice.
