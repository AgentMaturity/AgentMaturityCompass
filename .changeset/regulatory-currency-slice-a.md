Industry sector packs now record regulatory currency: each pack carries a content `version` (`2026.10`) and a `lastReviewed` date, and its regulatory basis and compliance frameworks resolve to a catalogue of instruments with a status, key dates and, where a source was checked, the official URL and retrieval date. Every pack now has at least 15 questions, so the 41 packs hold 632 sector questions (was 600; 876 with the 244 default questions), and several pack citations are corrected. Adds a sourced regulatory register (`src/compliance/regulatory/register.json`: 17 instruments, 11 verified, 6 unverified) and `npm run check:regulatory-currency`, which fails when an entry is malformed, cites a host outside the register's official-host list or was last reviewed more than 90 days ago; CI runs it. EU AI Act high-risk dates follow Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III systems from 2 December 2027 and Annex I systems from 2 August 2028 (`EU_AI_ACT_TIMELINE`). `DpiaAssessment` review dates may now be `null`: the DPIA template no longer stamps review dates or DPO approval it never recorded. Legacy `GLOBAL_FRAMEWORKS` entries report `mappingStatus: "partial"` and name their register entry. Regulatory feeds that are not live official sources are disabled as manual-review-required. The library also exports `normalizeComplianceFrameworkLabel` and the `RegulatoryReference` type. The catalogue, register and citations are agent-drafted and stay experimental until a named expert reviews them; they are not legal advice, and AMC output is evidence of conformity, not a certification.
