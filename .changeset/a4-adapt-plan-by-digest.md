A4 Adapt crosses on the shipped catalog (P2-42), behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `POST …/stages/adapt/propose` answered 413 `INPUT_TOO_LARGE` for every project on the shipped catalog: the compiled plan (about 140 to 150 KiB) was carried twice, in Understand's facts and in the proposal, past the room the 256 KiB specification limit leaves for Build. Each Adapt revision row now binds its catalog-sized parts by a ref (`amc.a4-derived-ref/v1`: `sha256` of the canonical bytes; kind `compiled-plan` with the plan's own `digest` for the plan, kind `json` for Understand's requirements, register entries and clock candidates, the explanation's facts and Review's two control lists), and each part is written once, after the release secret scan, to `a4-projects/<projectId>/derived/<sha256>.json`. Readiness, the producers, Build, Review and activation read the parts back checked against their refs; a missing file, other bytes, a ref that does not parse or one of the wrong kind is 409 `A4_INTEGRITY_FAILED`, never an empty part, and `verifyA4Chain` (`GET …/verify`, `amc verify all`, the binder slice) reports `A4_DERIVED_MISSING`, `A4_DERIVED_MISMATCH` or `A4_DERIVED_REF_INVALID`. `GET …/revisions/:n` answers the stored form, which hashes to `specDigest`; the new `GET …/revisions/:n/resolved` (`amc.a4-revision-resolved/v1`) answers the resolved spec, checked the same way, and the Studio A4 pages read it. An exported `amc.a4-record/v1` carries the ref and still verifies without a key; the offline report warns `DERIVED_BOUND_BY_DIGEST`. Revisions recorded before this change keep their inline plan and are read as they are. The 256 KiB limit is unchanged: an Adapt row is now about 6 to 15 KiB as stored (the proposal was 321 to 401 KiB on one station), and a project on all seven stations crosses Review. A restore of the ledger without `a4-projects/` leaves Adapt unreadable; `amc backup create` carries that directory only when the signed ops policy's `backups.includePaths` lists it, and a WORM export never does (docs/A4_FORGE.md, "What carries `a4-projects/`").
