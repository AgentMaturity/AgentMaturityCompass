A4 Adapt crosses on the shipped catalog (P2-42), behind the A4 preview (`AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`). `POST …/stages/adapt/propose` answered 413 `INPUT_TOO_LARGE` for every project on the shipped catalog: the compiled plan (about 140 to 150 KiB) was carried twice, in Understand's facts and in the proposal, past the room the 256 KiB specification limit leaves for Build. Each Adapt revision row now binds the plan by a ref (`amc.a4-derived-ref/v1`: `sha256` of the plan's canonical bytes and the plan's own `digest`), and the plan is written once, after the release secret scan, to `a4-projects/<projectId>/derived/<sha256>.json`. Readiness, the producers, Build, Review and activation read the plan back checked against the ref; a missing file, other bytes or a ref that does not parse is 409 `A4_INTEGRITY_FAILED`, never an empty plan, and `verifyA4Chain` (`GET …/verify`, `amc verify all`, the binder slice) reports `A4_DERIVED_MISSING`, `A4_DERIVED_MISMATCH` or `A4_DERIVED_REF_INVALID`. `GET …/revisions/:n` answers the stored form, which hashes to `specDigest`. An exported `amc.a4-record/v1` carries the ref and still verifies without a key; the offline report warns `DERIVED_BOUND_BY_DIGEST`. Revisions recorded before this change keep their inline plan and are read as they are. The 256 KiB limit is unchanged: a proposal on one station is now 73 to 103 KiB as stored (it was 321 to 401 KiB); a project on all seven stations is still refused at Review.
