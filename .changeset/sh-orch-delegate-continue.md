Kept-open delegates and `delegate_continue` (SH-ORCH-05). `delegate({runAs, goal, keepOpen: true})` keeps an in-process delegate after its answer; `delegate_continue({runAs, text})` sends it more work and returns its own words, and `delegate_continue({runAs, release: true})` releases it. A continuation is one more executor turn of the same delegation: the same signed packet, `timeout-ms` lifetime and `max-turns` budget, the same `--max-active-children` slot (held until release), and `agent_delegation_completed` deferred until the delegate is released. A kept-open delegate lives no longer than its caller's toolset: the end of the run, or the release of a delegated caller, releases it before the caller's session is sealed, and shutdown waits for a delegate still being kept. `keepOpen` is offered only to in-process delegates (never under `--delegate-provider`) and only while the signed tools policy lists `delegate_continue` (action class `READ_ONLY`); otherwise it is refused before any packet is signed. Two `delegate` calls in one model step already ran concurrently; docs/NATIVE_DELEGATION_LIMITS.md now documents that bound beside the new continuation.
