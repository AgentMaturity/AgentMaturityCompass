Trusted time with RFC 3161 timestamps (P1-25). Claimed time and attested time are now kept apart. Operators can pin timestamp authorities in a signed trust list (`timestampAuthorities`) and name TSAs in `.amc/amc.config.yaml` (`time.tsa`, `time.required`, `time.toleranceMinutes`, `time.checkpoint`). With TSAs configured, the Studio scheduler writes signed ledger checkpoints under `.amc/time/checkpoints/`, records a `time/checkpoint` ledger event once a token verifies, and leaves checkpoints pending while no TSA answers; `amc certify` embeds `timestamps/<sha256>.tsr` over `cert.json` and lists it in `metadata/exportInfo.json`. With `time.required: true`, checkpoints and certificate issuance fail without a verified token. Tokens are verified offline against the pinned anchors only (CMS signature, signing-certificate binding, critical id-kp-timeStamping, path and validity at genTime); revocation is not checked and every attested time says `revocationChecked: false`. `VerifierReportV1` gains an optional `time` field: `amc cert verify` reports the claimed issue time, any attested time and its basis, and `freshness` fails with `BACKDATED_CLAIM` or `POSTDATED_CLAIM` when the claim lies outside the attested window. A token proves that a hash existed by genTime, not that the content is true. Requests go only to configured TSAs, through the egress host decision, without redirects. Without `time.tsa` nothing changes. No dependency was added.
