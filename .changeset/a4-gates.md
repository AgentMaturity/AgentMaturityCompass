A4 Forge preview, gates and readiness (P1-57 slice A; the routes are in the `a4-router` changeset). The `/api/v1/a4` route family is registered, and every path answers 404 `A4_PREVIEW_DISABLED` unless the process runs with `AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`. Before that check the entry refuses a request without native Studio admission (401) and the demo session on every method (403 `DEMO_REFUSED`); after it, the admin token reads only (403 `ADMIN_TOKEN_REFUSED` on every mutation) and Studio's read-only mode refuses mutations (403 `NATIVE_READ_ONLY`).

- Approvals: `evaluateApprovalQuorum` takes an optional `now`. An approval request may carry a `quorumFloor` that only raises the signed class rule (more approvals, distinct users, fewer roles, a TTL capped by the signed policy's new optional `a4.maxTtlMinutes`); `signPlan` passes it to the plan-review request. The signed approval policy accepts an optional `a4` floor section (`regulated`, `allowSelfApproval`, `allowedGateChanges`, TTL bounds); existing policies parse and verify unchanged. A Studio approval decision bound to a request digest is refused with 403 when the reviewer has no verified user id or no roles, and never defaults to `OWNER`; unbound decisions are unchanged.
- A4 store: creating a project needs a live `OPERATOR` or `OWNER` role (403 `PRINCIPAL_ROLE_INSUFFICIENT`); membership changes need a current project owner on live roles (403 `A4_NOT_A_MEMBER`) and owner mode; a ledger evidence ref whose signed row names another agent, or none, is refused (409 `EVIDENCE_REF_FOREIGN_AGENT`), and the row is now resolved under the project lock; a gate-policy change below the D-16 defaults or the signed `a4` floor is refused (409 `GATE_POLICY_BELOW_FLOOR`), and one that loosens the policy in force needs owner mode and a current owner. One store commit may record several chained transitions, so a consumed gate and its started effect land in one transaction with the effect's liveness row.
- Gates (`src/a4/a4Gates.ts`, `src/a4/a4Effects.ts`): documentary gates are approval requests whose floors come from the live signed approval policy and the project's gate policy, binding the A4 intent (specification and resource digests, members, evidence refs, gate policy, excluded principals and a readiness digest over the stage's bound items). Decisions are refused on a stale request digest, a moved intent (409 `A4_GATE_STALE` naming what moved), drifted resources (409 `RESOURCE_DRIFTED`), an expiring gate, a second vote, a regulated project's host-session identity, or a separation-of-duties violation (400 `SOD_VIOLATION`); every decision records its identity provenance, the self-approval facts it was derived from and the bound items its approver saw, and stays `self_reported`, never an independent review. A gate is consumed once. An active incident freeze writes an automatic hold by `amc-runtime` naming the incident and refuses; resume needs the freeze lifted. Effect gates are separate engine requests (two distinct approvers when regulated) whose counted decisions are re-checked against A4's separation of duties and project membership before consumption; effects run after commit, re-check the freeze, read-only mode and every bound resource first, heartbeat every 5 seconds, and are settled `process_lost` only when no executor in their owner process is still running them, when their owner process is gone on this host, or when it cannot be checked (another host) and has stopped heartbeating; a running executor is never settled, however long a step runs.
- Readiness (`src/a4/a4Readiness.ts`) is one pure evaluator: governance items, a non-claim integrity section, owner acknowledgements that keep an item WAITING (`OWNER_ACKNOWLEDGED`, 90 days), per-principal permissions, and one claim envelope folded from the evidence refs and decisions (gate decisions keep it `self_reported`). A regulated project is one whose brief risk tier is anything other than `low`, `med` or `medium` (unstated, unrecognised and differently spelled tiers included), whose head Adapt revision carries an activated plan, or whose workspace floor says so; single-user self-approval is derived, ratchets off for good, and never applies to a regulated project or the policy gate. Two local users are not evidence of two people.
- Review fixes. Every A4 transition records the principal population it was written under, so a project that ever had two or more active principals during a write never self-approves again, and neither does one whose `users.yaml` holds a user created after the project (revoked or not). A regulated project's `sod.self_provisioned` item waits whenever the project has approvers, since host-session decisions are refused there, so `complete` needs the owner acknowledgement; a regulated effect refuses a host-session engine decision (403 `IDENTITY_CHECK_LIMITED`). A signed config that was absent when the revision was proposed and exists later is `RESOURCE_DRIFTED`. An effect gate opens only on the gate its effect completes, under that gate's stage (409 `A4_STEP_ORDER`). After an effect whose executor consumes its own grant fails and an owner re-opens its effect gate, `complete` on the consumed gate runs it again (a new attempt; the gate is not consumed twice). A failed effect stops blocking once a REOPEN or TUNE returns to its stage or earlier, or a new revision of its stage is written; a lost attempt reads BLOCKED `PROCESS_LOST` with retry as the next action. A gate, the policy gate included, is not requested while an effect is running or the notary is in its retry window. A draft's creator may retire it until it reaches `proposed`, answers included. `complete` answers an integrity failure with 409 `A4_NOT_READY` (`A4_INTEGRITY_FAILED`), as GET readiness shows it, rather than a stale gate. A policy gate bars only its requester, not the head revision's author. A gate superseded by a later transition answers 409 `A4_GATE_STALE` with `moved` naming the slot (`memberSet`, `evidenceRefs`, `readiness`, `spec`, `policy` or `consumed`). The automatic freeze hold is mirrored to the human action log and the `A4_HOLD` event like a manual one. Inside the ledger transaction, a governed write reuses the rows it verified just before and reads again the live facts, its evidence refs' resolution against the ledger and the trust list, and an effect's engine decisions.
- Second review fixes. A gate policy's `requiredReviews` are enforced: `complete`, and the consumption of a policy gate, are refused with 409 `A4_NOT_READY` (`REQUIRED_REVIEW_PENDING`) until every entry of the rule in force when the gate was requested is satisfied, and readiness shows the item `gate.required_reviews` (WAITING) meanwhile. A `role_vote` entry is satisfied by a counted approval whose recorded roles hold the role, which is an acknowledgement and not a review. A `review_record` entry is satisfied by a verified-lane ref on the gate's revision. With the D-16 defaults, Adapt completion therefore waits for an approval from a project approver or owner holding `AUDITOR`; an `AUDITOR` without `APPROVER` or `OWNER` may cast it, and the gate's own allowed roles keep an auditor off `WRITE_*`, `DEPLOY` and policy gates (403 `PRINCIPAL_ROLE_INSUFFICIENT`). A4 reads the approval policy's rules, its `a4` floor and its signature verdict from one read. An unverified policy lends nothing: readiness shows the default rules under the strictest floor with `approvals.policy_signed` BLOCKED, and opening an effect gate or changing the gate policy is refused with 409 `APPROVAL_POLICY_MISSING` or `APPROVAL_POLICY_UNSIGNED`. An effect whose executor ends inside a running Studio without writing its outcome reads as lost (`PROCESS_LOST`), and retry settles it. A failed effect is retried at most once on its gate; a second failure is 409 `EFFECT_NOT_RETRYABLE` and needs a new revision of the stage. It is never retried after a REOPEN, TUNE or new revision of its stage (409 `A4_GATE_STALE`), and the same rule stops the re-open and re-run of an effect that consumes its own grant. That re-run is refused with 409 `RESOURCE_DRIFTED` before any attempt is written when a bound resource moved. A policy gate can be decided while the policy in force sits below a newly signed `a4` floor, and new projects start with gate TTLs inside the signed `a4` TTL bounds. A host session issued after a project was created turns its self-approval off, even once the session expires or is revoked. A signed config that exists but cannot be read is `RESOURCE_DRIFTED`, and a revision or gate request that would bind it is refused with 409 `RESOURCE_UNREADABLE`.
- Third review fixes. An effect gate whose engine request closed (`QUORUM_MET` or `DENIED`) on a decision A4 never counts can be re-opened by an owner. Such a decision is an approval A4 would refuse at `complete` (unresolvable, not a project approver or owner, recorded roles beyond the live ones, a host session on a regulated project, or a separation-of-duties violation), or a deny from a principal who does not resolve or is not a project approver or owner. A deny from a valid member still keeps the effect gate closed (409 `EFFECT_GATE_OPEN`). A REOPEN, TUNE or new revision of an effect's stage that lands while the attempt is still running now also stops its retry, re-run and re-open (409 `A4_GATE_STALE`), and readiness clears that attempt's failure once it settles. A running attempt still reads `BUILD_RUNNING`. A retry whose engine grant was never consumed (the attempt was lost before it started) checks that grant as `complete` does. An expired, denied or cancelled grant gets 409 `EFFECT_QUORUM_INSUFFICIENT`, an approver who was revoked or removed gets 400 `SOD_VIOLATION`, and a host session on a regulated project gets 403 `IDENTITY_CHECK_LIMITED`. In each case nothing is written. An approval that would meet a gate's quorum while a required `role_vote` that the approver cannot cast is still pending is refused with 409 `A4_NOT_READY` (`REQUIRED_REVIEW_PENDING`), so the required role always keeps a vote: under the D-16 defaults, two `OWNER` approvals no longer close Adapt completion before the `AUDITOR` votes. A brief that states `riskTier` both at its top level and under `brief` makes the project regulated unless the two values agree. On a regulated project, GET readiness gives `allowed.decide` the reason `IDENTITY_CHECK_LIMITED` for a host-session principal, as the decision route refuses it. `allowed.decide` still leaves out the gate's separation-of-duties exclusions, because an excluded principal may deny. Their approval is refused when it is written. Inside the ledger transaction, a governed write no longer re-walks the ledger chain for each ledger evidence ref: it reuses that row's chain verdict from the check made just before the lock, for the same row id and hash, and still re-reads the row, its digest, pruning, tier and trust-list admission.
- Fourth review fixes. An effect whose executor returned is never recorded `EFFECT_FAILED`: its `EFFECT_FINISHED` row is written once more after a store error, and if that also fails the attempt stays unsettled and reads as lost (`PROCESS_LOST`), as an effect with an unknown outcome. A valid project approver's or owner's deny keeps an effect gate closed (409 `EFFECT_GATE_OPEN`) even when the engine request also holds an approval A4 would refuse; a `DENIED` request re-opens only when every deny on it is one A4 never counts. Approve and deny on an A4 gate take `APPROVER`, `AUDITOR` or `OWNER`, as Studio's engine route does, and the gate's own allowed roles still refuse an auditor on `WRITE_*`, `DEPLOY` and policy gates (403 `PRINCIPAL_ROLE_INSUFFICIENT`), so a project approver who holds only `AUDITOR` can cast the vote Adapt completion requires under the D-16 defaults. GET readiness reports `BLOCKED`, not `COMPLETE`, for a stage whose gates are consumed while a mandatory item is blocked (a failed or lost effect, an active freeze, an unsigned approval policy, drifted resources), and `WAITING` while its effect runs or the freeze cannot be read.
