A4 Forge preview, gates and readiness (P1-57 slice A; the routes are in the `a4-router` changeset). The `/api/v1/a4` route family is registered, and every path answers 404 `A4_PREVIEW_DISABLED` unless the process runs with `AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`. Before that check the entry refuses a request without native Studio admission (401) and the demo session on every method (403 `DEMO_REFUSED`); after it, the admin token reads only (403 `ADMIN_TOKEN_REFUSED` on every mutation) and Studio's read-only mode refuses mutations (403 `NATIVE_READ_ONLY`).

- Approvals: `evaluateApprovalQuorum` takes an optional `now`. An approval request may carry a `quorumFloor` that only raises the signed class rule (more approvals, distinct users, fewer roles, a TTL capped by the signed policy's new optional `a4.maxTtlMinutes`); `signPlan` passes it to the plan-review request. The signed approval policy accepts an optional `a4` floor section (`regulated`, `allowSelfApproval`, `allowedGateChanges`, TTL bounds); existing policies parse and verify unchanged. A Studio approval decision bound to a request digest is refused with 403 when the reviewer has no verified user id or no roles, and never defaults to `OWNER`; unbound decisions are unchanged.
- A4 store: creating a project needs a live `OPERATOR` or `OWNER` role (403 `PRINCIPAL_ROLE_INSUFFICIENT`); membership changes need a current project owner on live roles (403 `A4_NOT_A_MEMBER`) and owner mode; a ledger evidence ref whose signed row names another agent, or none, is refused (409 `EVIDENCE_REF_FOREIGN_AGENT`), and the row is now resolved under the project lock; a gate-policy change below the D-16 defaults or the signed `a4` floor is refused (409 `GATE_POLICY_BELOW_FLOOR`), and one that loosens the policy in force needs owner mode and a current owner. One store commit may record several chained transitions, so a consumed gate and its started effect land in one transaction with the effect's liveness row.
- Gates (`src/a4/a4Gates.ts`, `src/a4/a4Effects.ts`): documentary gates are approval requests whose floors come from the live signed approval policy and the project's gate policy, binding the A4 intent (specification and resource digests, members, evidence refs, gate policy, excluded principals and a readiness digest over the stage's bound items). Decisions are refused on a stale request digest, a moved intent (409 `A4_GATE_STALE` naming what moved), drifted resources (409 `RESOURCE_DRIFTED`), an expiring gate, a second vote, a regulated project's host-session identity, or a separation-of-duties violation (400 `SOD_VIOLATION`); every decision records its identity provenance, the self-approval facts it was derived from and the bound items its approver saw, and stays `self_reported`, never an independent review. A gate is consumed once. An active incident freeze writes an automatic hold by `amc-runtime` naming the incident and refuses; resume needs the freeze lifted. Effect gates are separate engine requests (two distinct approvers when regulated) whose counted decisions are re-checked against A4's separation of duties and project membership before consumption; effects run after commit, re-check the freeze, read-only mode and every bound resource first, heartbeat every 5 seconds, and are settled `process_lost` only when their owner process is gone on this host, or cannot be checked (another host) and has stopped heartbeating; a live local executor is never settled, however long a step runs.
- Readiness (`src/a4/a4Readiness.ts`) is one pure evaluator: governance items, a non-claim integrity section, owner acknowledgements that keep an item WAITING (`OWNER_ACKNOWLEDGED`, 90 days), per-principal permissions, and one claim envelope folded from the evidence refs and decisions (gate decisions keep it `self_reported`). A regulated project is one whose brief risk tier is high, critical or unstated, whose head Adapt revision carries an activated plan, or whose workspace floor says so; single-user self-approval is derived, ratchets off for good, and never applies to a regulated project or the policy gate. Two local users are not evidence of two people.
- Review fixes. Every A4 transition records the principal population it was written under, so a project that ever had two or more active principals during a write never self-approves again, and neither does one whose `users.yaml` holds a user created after the project (revoked or not). A regulated project's `sod.self_provisioned` item waits whenever the project has approvers, since host-session decisions are refused there, so `complete` needs the owner acknowledgement; a regulated effect refuses a host-session engine decision (403 `IDENTITY_CHECK_LIMITED`). A signed config that was absent when the revision was proposed and exists later is `RESOURCE_DRIFTED`. An effect gate opens only on the gate its effect completes, under that gate's stage (409 `A4_STEP_ORDER`). After an effect whose executor consumes its own grant fails and an owner re-opens its effect gate, `complete` on the consumed gate runs it again (a new attempt; the gate is not consumed twice). A failed effect stops blocking once a REOPEN or TUNE returns to its stage or earlier, or a new revision of its stage is written; a lost attempt reads BLOCKED `PROCESS_LOST` with retry as the next action. A gate is not requested while an effect is running or the notary is in its retry window. A draft's creator may retire it until it reaches `proposed`, answers included. `complete` answers an integrity failure with 409 `A4_NOT_READY` (`A4_INTEGRITY_FAILED`), as GET readiness shows it, rather than a stale gate. A policy gate bars only its requester, not the head revision's author. A gate superseded by a later transition answers 409 `A4_GATE_STALE` with `moved` naming the slot (`memberSet`, `evidenceRefs`, `readiness`, `spec`, `policy` or `consumed`). The automatic freeze hold is mirrored to the human action log and the `A4_HOLD` event like a manual one. Inside the ledger transaction, a governed write re-checks only live facts and an effect's engine decisions, on the rows it verified just before.
