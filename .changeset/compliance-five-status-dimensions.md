Breaking: compliance results now report five status dimensions; partial and unknown credit removed. Every compliance category carries `dimensions` (applicability, evidence, result, enforcement, review), a derived `claimKind`, `claimReasons`, and the `admitted` and `rejected` evidence refs with a reason for each rejection (`producer_not_admitted`, `producer_unverified`, `binding_missing`, `binding_mismatch`, `cross_tenant`, `outside_window`, `stale`, `replayed`, `invalidated`). The engine never writes `PARTIAL` or `UNKNOWN`: a failed requirement fails the category (`MISSING`), and `status` is kept for one more minor release, derived from the result (pass `SATISFIED`, fail `MISSING`, not evaluated `NOT_EVALUATED`). The coverage score credits passes only; `partial` and `unknown` stay in the JSON at 0. The `countedObserved` field is removed.

Each mapping requirement now runs through the catalog evidence evaluator (`src/catalog/evidence/`). Ledger records count only when the whole hash chain verifies and its head carries the workspace's monitor-key signature, read in the same pass; a record whose receipt does not verify, or commits to another record, is rejected. This is a local audit trail checked against the workspace's own key, not a portable verdict. Records that name another tenant, records more than 30 days older than the window end, and imported, manual or synthetic records are rejected; when nothing is admitted and a record failed for its producer or tenant, evidence is `untrusted`. Allow and deny records for one tool call id make the evidence `contradictory`. A denied audit event that was not admitted (an import, for example) still blocks a pass. The claim kind is observed only when every admitted record derives OBSERVED, and the old trust-tier helper that read the stored tier for compliance is gone.

No compiled plan records yet whether a control applies to a deployment, so applicability is `unresolved` and a category with sufficient evidence is still not evaluated. Until the applicability compiler ships, a compliance report can show a failure but no pass, and the coverage score stays null unless a category failed. Older ledger rows that lack binding fields stay unadmitted; that is the intended result, not a regression. The Markdown report, coverage matrix (new "Framework Claims" section; the partial and unknown columns are gone), CLI and the unified Comply surface read the result and the dimensions; the CLI prints `Coverage: not evaluated (0 of N categories passed or failed)`. The producer registry adds `amc.assuranceRunner` for sealed assurance reports. A compliance report is evidence of conformity, not a certification.
