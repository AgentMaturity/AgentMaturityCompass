A4 Forge routes (P1-57 slice B, preview). With `AMC_A4_PREVIEW=1`, Studio serves `/api/v1/a4` to signed-in workspace users through native admission. Without the variable every path answers 404 `A4_PREVIEW_DISABLED`. Release, deployment and rollback routes answer 404 `A4_ROUTE_NOT_FOUND` until P1-62.

- Who may call: the admin token reads and is refused on every mutation, project creation included (403 `ADMIN_TOKEN_REFUSED`); the demo session is refused on every route (403 `DEMO_REFUSED`); Studio's read-only mode refuses every mutation (403 `NATIVE_READ_ONLY`). Approve and deny take the approve class (APPROVER or OWNER), request-changes takes APPROVER, AUDITOR or OWNER, and hold, resume, reopen, tune, retire, members, gate-policy, acknowledge, effects and Adapt/Activate completion take OWNER. Comments and presence take any read role plus project membership. Project reads need membership, except for workspace OWNERs, AUDITORs and the admin token. A body that names `userId`, `roles`, `approver`, `author`, `approved`, `approvalId`, `token` or a trust-list field is refused with 400; member roles are sent as `projectRoles`.
- Projects: `POST /projects` (OPERATOR or OWNER; a project for an agent that already exists needs OWNER), `GET /projects`, `GET /projects/:id` (head, members, current revision, gates and readiness), `GET /inbox`, `GET /options`, revisions, a revision diff, transitions, gates, members, comments (bodies are read from the encrypted blob store), evidence refs, a cursor poll with presence (`…/events`), owner holds and acknowledgements, and `POST …/evidence` (a ledger row reaches the observed lane only when its recomputed trust tier is observed; everything else is self-reported).
- Stages: answers, understand, confirm-understanding, explain, propose, build and review follow the fixed step order. No stage has a producer yet, so understand, explain, build and review store the human-written text encrypted in the project's blob store, sign it as an `a4-stage-output` artifact, and record it as a self-reported implementation-lane evidence ref. Review records its observed checks as not evaluated (`NO_PRODUCER_REGISTERED`). Gates are requested, approved, denied, sent back for changes and consumed through `…/complete`. Effect gates are opened and retried under `…/effects/:effectId`, and a hypothesis is observed through `…/hypotheses/:id/observe`. Every mutation carries a `clientRequestId`: a retry answers the recorded result, and a reused id with another body is 409 `REQUEST_CONFLICT`. A stale head is 409 `A4_STALE_HEAD` with the head and a transitions link.
- Claims: `GET /projects/:id`, `…/readiness`, `…/conformance`, `…/value`, `…/monitor` and `…/verify` are result routes whose wrapper repeats the record's own `claim` (`ClaimSource` `a4_record`); readiness, conformance and value are regulated. Value, monitor and conformance answer `not_evaluated` until their producers land. `GET …/verify` takes no query parameters and runs `verifyA4Chain` under the server operator's trust list. It reports integrity of bytes under this workspace's keys, which is not evidence about the agent.
- `amc verify all` gains the `a4-projects` check, one row per project: a changed transition body fails with `A4_CHAIN_INVALID`, and a missing or extra side row fails with `A4_SIDE_ROW_MISMATCH`. Other failures are a decision not bound to its gate's request digest (`freshness: fail`; it counts for nothing), a ref whose stored trust tier differs from the referenced row's (`TRUST_TIER_INFLATED`), a self-approval the chain does not derive, and a credential in a stored request response. A ledger that predates migration 13 skips the check, and one at or after it without the A4 tables fails. Readiness on GET and at `complete` carries this verifier report in its integrity section.
- Audit: gate requests, decisions and holds are mirrored to the human action log and sent as notification-only integration events (`A4_GATE_OPENED`, `A4_GATE_DECIDED`, `A4_HOLD`). `amc mode agent` blocks `a4 acknowledge`.
- OpenAPI: `website/openapi.yaml` publishes the A4 routes and request schemas (`node scripts/update-native-task-openapi.mjs` after a build), with `ClaimResult` on the result routes.
