Adds OWASP ACS v0.1 hook support for `steps/toolCallRequest`, `handshake/hello` and `system/ping` on AMC's existing decision path; ASI mapping is experimental (P2-19). The Bridge serves `POST /bridge/hooks/acs/v0.1` on loopback to agents holding an AMC hook lease with the `hook:control` scope and the `/hooks/acs/v0.1` route. A tool call is decided by the same signed ToolHub, Action Policy and Approval Policy path as Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Pi hooks, binds the P1-02 authorization record on an allow, and returns AMC's signed receipt in `policy_data.amc`; the receipt records advisory enforcement, because AMC cannot see whether a third-party runtime honours its answer. An approval AMC cannot route is answered `deny` with `approver_unavailable`, every other ACS hook is answered "unsupported" (never allow), and AMC claims no ACS conformance: it verifies no ACS signatures and publishes no ACS chain hash. Pinned to ACS release v0.1.4; see docs/standards/ACS.md. Also adds an experimental OWASP Agentic Top 10 (ASI) crosswalk at docs/standards/crosswalks/owasp-asi-2026.json, with ASI05 and ASI07 listed as gaps, and draft ACS test cases for OWASP under docs/standards/acs-contributions/ (not submitted).
