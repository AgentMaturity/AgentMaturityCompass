Compliance guide

Line up what your agent did against a framework such as the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF, SOC 2 or the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, and see which parts have records behind them. AMC does not certify that you comply, and none of this is legal advice.

2027 Annex III timeline

EU AI Act — current high-risk timeline

The European Commission's AI Act page gives 2 December 2027 for the high-risk rules in sensitive areas (Annex III) and 2 August 2028 for AI built into regulated products, after changes made by the AI Omnibus. The Frameworks page lists every date with its source. Check the Commission's page for later changes.

EU AI Act Compliance

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) sets duties by risk. amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT lines up your agent's records against its high-risk duties and says, for each one, whether there are records behind it. In a new folder every line reads "not evaluated", because nothing has been recorded yet.

# Generate EU AI Act compliance report
amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT --window 30d --out reports/eu-ai-act.md

# Quick EU AI Act risk classification
amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT

# Export compliance evidence binder
amc audit binder create --scope workspace --out binder.amcaudit
Important disclaimer A report shows which duties have records behind them. It is not a certificate. A signature on the report shows who made it and that it is unchanged, not that the records are enough or true; use a report outside your team only when its evidence readiness reads READY. AMC does not certify legal compliance. Always consult qualified legal counsel. See the European Commission high-risk guidance.

Risk level

Each Industry Pack carries a risk level that AMC suggests (high, very high or critical) for an agent in that area. A project started from a pack in A4 Forge uses it to set who must approve each step: every one of the 41 packs is high risk or above, so someone other than you approves. Whether the EU AI Act counts your agent as high-risk is a legal question for your own counsel; AMC does not decide it.

Compliance Workflow

1

Classify your agent

amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT

The report lists each duty and whether records support it.

2

Apply domain packs

amc domain list                              # See all 7 domains + 41 packs
amc domain assess --domain health --agent my-agent  # Run domain assessment
3

Find compliance gaps

amc domain gaps --domain health --agent my-agent
amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT --window 30d
4

Fix and verify

amc fix                         # Write fix files for the gaps found
amc guide --compliance EU_AI_ACT  # Map gaps to regulatory obligations
5

Export audit evidence

amc audit binder create --scope workspace --out binder.amcaudit  # Generate compliance binder
amc bundle export --run latest --out evidence.amcbundle  # Portable evidence bundle

Both sign what they write, so they need your vault passphrase in that shell.

7 Domains — 41 Industry Packs

Industry Packs add questions for one area of work to AMC's 244 default questions, and name the rules that area works under. AMC has 41 sector-specific packs across 7 domains, all free in the current source build, with no licence, account or payment (the published 1.1.1 release still asks for a licence). They are self-reported questionnaires whose content is experimental: AI-drafted until a named expert signs it.

amc domain list                                   # See all 7 domains + packs
amc domain assess --domain health --agent my-agent  # Assess against a domain
amc domain gaps --domain wealth --agent my-agent    # Find compliance gaps
amc domain report --domain environment --output env.md  # Generate report

Other Compliance Frameworks

ISO 42001

AI management system standard. Clauses 4–10 mapped to AMC dimensions.

amc compliance report --framework ISO_42001

NIST AI RMF

Risk management framework alignment for US federal and enterprise.

amc compliance report --framework NIST_AI_RMF

SOC 2

Trust service criteria mapping for service organization controls.

amc compliance report --framework SOC2

OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications

Lines up records against the ten risks of the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, which is community guidance rather than a law.

amc compliance report --framework OWASP_LLM

Audit Binder

Generate a portable, signed evidence package for auditors:

# Create a signed audit binder for the workspace
amc audit binder create --scope workspace --out binder.amcaudit

# List existing binders
amc audit binder list

# Verify a binder, pinning the issuer's auditor key recorded outside the binder
amc audit binder verify binder.amcaudit --pubkey auditor.pub

# Export for external sharing (controlled access)
amc audit binder export-request
amc audit binder export-execute
Every Industry Pack names a suggested risk level, the rules its area works under and its main risks. Pack content is experimental and AI-drafted, not reviewed by a qualified expert, and not legal advice.
