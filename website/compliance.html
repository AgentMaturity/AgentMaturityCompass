Frameworks

See which rules your agent's records can support

AMC lines up the records it kept of your agent's work with the duties in the EU AI Act and 21 other frameworks. It shows which duties those records relate to, and which ones nothing supports yet.

Frameworks
22, from the EU AI Act and GDPR to ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF.
What you get
A map from duties to records, with the gaps listed. Not a certificate.
Who decides
AMC does not certify legal compliance. A qualified lawyer decides which duties apply to you.

What a result means

A document that says a control exists does not show that it worked. AMC can watch the agent as it works, sign a record of each action (a receipt), and link each duty back to those receipts.

  • A VALID file shows who signed it and that it has not changed. It does not show there are enough records, or that they are true.
  • Only a readiness result of READY can back a claim to someone else. Until AMC has recorded your agent at work, a duty reads not evaluated.
  • A level is not a legal status. No level from L0 to L5 means you meet a law. Today a question can reach L2 at most: see how the score works.

Evidence Mapping, Not Certification

Some EU AI Act articles, and what AMC's records can and cannot add to each.

Article 9: Risk management

Receipts from attack tests can help you find risks and review how you reduce them. Whether they apply, and whether they are enough, depends on your system.

Article 12: Record keeping

AMC links each event it records or imports to the one before it with a fingerprint (a hash chain) and signs it. Activity AMC did not record is not in its log.

Article 13: Transparency

Reports show levels, links to the records behind them, and what nothing supports yet. A reviewer can check AMC files offline with amc verify all --json --expect-monitor <fingerprint>, which names in advance a monitor key kept outside the workspace. Without it the log check says UNANCHORED.

Article 14: Human oversight

Signed rules for actions, and A4 Forge's approvals, can show where a person must approve. On their own they do not show legal conformity.

Article 17: Quality management

Monitoring, trial runs of new rules beside the current ones, and alerts when behaviour changes can support a review, if you set them up and they are running.

EU AI Act: when its rules apply

The dates the European Commission's AI Act page gives. The AI Omnibus is a later law that changed some of them.

  1. 1 August 2024: the AI Act entered into force.
  2. 2 February 2025: the bans on prohibited AI practices and the AI literacy duties started to apply.
  3. 2 August 2025: the governance rules and the duties for general-purpose AI models started to apply.
  4. 27 July 2026: the AI Omnibus entered into force.
  5. 2 August 2026: the AI Act became applicable, with exceptions. The page says its transparency rules come into effect in August 2026.
  6. December 2026: a ninth prohibition, added by the AI Omnibus, comes into effect. It bans AI that makes non-consensual sexual images or child sexual abuse material.
  7. 2 December 2027: the high-risk rules apply to systems used in sensitive areas, such as biometrics, critical infrastructure, education and employment.
  8. 2 August 2028: the high-risk rules apply to AI built into regulated products, such as lifts or toys.

Source: the European Commission, AI Act (page last updated 19 October 2026, read 15 November 2026). Check it for later changes. This is not legal advice.

The 22 frameworks

Each one can be chosen with amc compliance report --framework <name>. The name to type is in brackets.

  • EU AI Act (EU_AI_ACT)
  • GDPR (GDPR)
  • ISO/IEC 42001 (ISO_42001)
  • ISO/IEC 27001 (ISO_27001)
  • NIST AI RMF (NIST_AI_RMF)
  • NIST AI 600-1, the generative AI profile (NIST_AI_600_1)
  • SOC 2 (SOC2)
  • HIPAA (HIPAA)
  • HHS HTI-1 (HHS_HTI_1)
  • PCI DSS (PCI_DSS)
  • SOX (SOX)
  • FedRAMP (FEDRAMP)
  • DORA (DORA)
  • NIS2 (NIS2)
  • Colorado AI law (CO_AI_ACT)
  • Texas TRAIGA (TX_TRAIGA)
  • California AI laws (CA_AI_LAWS)
  • Korea AI Basic Act (KR_AI_BASIC_ACT)
  • MITRE ATLAS (MITRE_ATLAS)
  • OWASP API Security Top 10 (OWASP_API_TOP10)
  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (OWASP_LLM)
  • OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (OWASP_AGENTIC)

The mappings are AMC's own reading of each framework. Some parts are marked unverified in the report where AMC could not read the source text. Industry Packs add the laws and standards of each sector: see Industries.

Try it

Build AMC from source first (see Get AMC). No account and no API key.

amc compliance report --framework EU_AI_ACT      # duties, the records behind each, and the gaps
amc audit binder create --scope workspace --out binder.amcaudit   # one signed file for a reviewer

Until AMC has recorded your agent at work, the report says not evaluated. It is never a legal certification.
