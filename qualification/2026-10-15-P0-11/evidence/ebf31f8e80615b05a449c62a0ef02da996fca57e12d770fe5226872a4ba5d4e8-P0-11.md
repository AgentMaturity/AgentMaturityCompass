## P0-11 · Write the threat model, channel coverage map and failure policy (A02)

**Gate** G0 · **Epic** E04 Native runtime: safety and parity floor · **Priority** High · **Due** 2026-10-25 · **Effort** M (1–3 person-weeks) · **Route** Agent-Doable · **Tags** Security, Documentation

**Blocked by** none · **Blocks** P1-02, P1-03, P1-05, P1-12, P2-01, GATE-G0 · **Old Linear issues** none · **Plan refs** backlog A02 "Threat model, channel coverage and failure policy"; strategy "How AMC makes a regulated claim › Failure behaviour" (L321–332) and "› Authorization record"; gaps G7, G23, G24, G25; "A02 and A03 constrain A04–A12"

### Why this matters

AMC may claim enforcement only at a boundary it controls, so every path by which an agent, tool or attacker can cause an effect or change evidence must be named with its enforcement point and evidence. Today enforcement is spread over fifteen channels of very different strength, with undocumented bypasses. P1-02, P1-03, P1-05, P1-12 and P2-01 need one normative map and one failure policy instead of inventing their own.

### Goal

`docs/security/THREAT_MODEL.md` and a machine-checked `docs/security/channel-map.json` name every asset, actor and channel with its enforcement point (file and symbol), evidence and gaps; the failure-behaviour table is normative, and every row points to a test or to the plan issue that implements it.

### Scope

**In:** the threat model document; the channel map and failure policy as JSON; a CI check that every cited path and symbol exists; STRIDE per channel; an OWASP Agentic Top 10 mapping; superseding the old document. **Out:** fixing any gap (each row names its owner); the authorization record (P1-02); receipt states (P1-03); the adversarial suite (P1-48).
