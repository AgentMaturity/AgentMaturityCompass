# P0-26 receipt: F5 drop review packet, decisions pending

Environment: local worktree on macOS arm64 (darwin 25.6.0), not CI.

## What this records

P0-26 asks Sid to decide keep, drop or defer for each of the 30 questions F5 (an AI editorial review of the station packs at `8f57ce63`) recommends dropping, and to choose a floor policy. Sid has not reviewed them yet, so this receipt records the packet and the guard, not a review:

- `decisions.json`: the decisions P1-42 reads. All 30 items are `defer` with the note "deferred: awaiting Sid"; `reviewedBy`, `reviewedAt` and `floorPolicy` are null; `proposedFloorPolicy` is the agent's proposal (`retire-floor`). It is identical to `docs/program/f5/decisions.json` (the guard test checks this).
- No question was dropped and no pack content, floor check or count test changed. The registry still has 41 packs and 632 questions, and the freeze baseline is unchanged.
- The packet `docs/program/f5-drop-review.md` (generated by `scripts/program/f5-drop-review.mjs` from `docs/program/f5/drops.json`) gives, for each item: the full question with L1, L3 and L5; the citation and its catalogue status; F5's issues verbatim; the meta-review note or "not sampled"; the pack count before and after and the floor flag; the question's score share; the station tags it carries alone; the tracked files that name it; the candidate commit that removed it; and the agent's draft recommendation.
- Agent draft recommendations (AI, not a decision, not reviewed by a qualified expert): keep 1 (WLT-NP-11), drop 28, defer 1 (EDU-HE-3).

## Sources

F5's per-station outputs (generated 2026-10-03T16:49Z at `8f57ce63d8331f1bef1c2a18fde82a7e8f4511da`) and its meta-review (2026-10-04, 60 sampled, 8 of them drops, all 8 agreed) are untracked files on the maintainer's workstation. The candidate's removal commits were verified with git: each id is present in the parent of its station's apply commit (`4f54445a`, `69be7333`, `4e53ceb5`, `ca7aec6a`, `febdc748`, `b6a7f6b1`, `ac8aca7f`) and absent after it, and no id appears under `src/` at `37c1466b`.

## Guard and mutation check

`tests/f5DropDecisions.test.ts`: one decision per F5 drop; a keep or drop only with `reviewedBy: "Sid"`, a date and a floor policy; every keep or defer id still in its pack and every drop id gone; the latest `qualification/*-P0-26/decisions.json` equals the docs copy. `mutations.txt` records four mutations (a deferred question removed as the candidate does, an unreviewed drop, a Sid-reviewed drop with the question still present, a receipt copy that drifts), each failing, plus a control where a reviewed drop with the question removed passes.

## When Sid decides

Edit `docs/program/f5/decisions.json`, re-run `node scripts/program/f5-drop-review.mjs`, copy the file into a new `qualification/<date>-P0-26/` with its own receipt, then apply the confirmed drops under the chosen floor policy (spec steps 6 to 8). A drop of MOB-F3W-8 must also edit `tests/logisticsIndustryPack.test.ts`, which names it.
