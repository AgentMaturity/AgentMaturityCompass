# BUYER_PACKAGES.md — Commercial offers by buyer type

The whole AMC product, the trust stack and all 41 Industry Packs included, is free and MIT licensed for every buyer type. The planned commercial offer is Enterprise: contact-first priority support, custom pack development and deployment assistance (`docs/PRICING.md`). No feature or pack depends on it.

## Startup / small AI team
Best fit:
- Free
- focus on Score, Shield, basic Watch

## Platform / engineering team
Best fit:
- Free
- focus on Score, Shield, Watch, some Fleet, selected Comply

## Regulated organization
Best fit:
- Free, with the Industry Packs for its sector; Enterprise (contact-first) for priority support, custom packs or deployment assistance
- focus on Shield, Enforce, Vault, Watch, Comply

## Enterprise security / governance team
Best fit:
- Free; Enterprise (contact-first) for priority support or deployment assistance
- focus on Enforce, Vault, Watch, Comply, Fleet, Passport

## OSS maintainer / ecosystem partner
Best fit:
- Free + sponsorship / ecosystem partnership path

## Why this page exists
To answer the buyer question: "what do we buy?" without making them infer it from raw feature lists.
