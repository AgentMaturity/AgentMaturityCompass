# PRICING_FAQ.md — Pricing Questions

## Is AMC free?
Yes. The full trust stack is MIT licensed and free: Score, Shield, Enforce, Vault, Watch, Comply, Fleet, Passport, all 15 adapters, 1,170 CLI command paths, browser playground, and CI gates.

## What costs money?
AMC plans to charge for **Industry Packs** — 41 sector-specific domain packs for regulated verticals like healthcare, finance, education, and government. Public checkout and automatic license issuance are not live yet.

## Do I need Industry Packs?
Not unless you work in a regulated industry and want pre-built, professionally maintained sector-specific diagnostics and compliance mappings. The core trust stack works perfectly without them.

## Can I build my own domain packs?
Yes. AMC's pack system is extensible. You can create custom packs for any domain. AMC intends to maintain the commercial Industry Packs with ongoing regulatory research once that channel launches.

## Will free features become paid later?
No. MIT license is permanent. The trust stack stays free.

## What is planned for Enterprise?
All 41 Industry Packs, priority support, custom pack development, and deployment assistance. This is contact-first planning, not a self-serve product claim.

## Is there a free trial for Industry Packs?
No public trial or checkout is live. Follow the repository's publication status before relying on commercial availability.
