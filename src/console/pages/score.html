Maturity Score

Scores run in the terminal; this page lists the commands. amc score --tier quick prints its result in the terminal only. A full assessment (amc run) is saved, and its result appears on the Agent page.

A score rates your agent's maturity across 5 layers with the default diagnostic question bank.

Commands: amc score --tier quick or amc score formal-spec <agentId>

See also: Compass View | Diagnostic Detail
